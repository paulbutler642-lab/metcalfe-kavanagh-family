What the file establishes

The file contains William’s signed voluntary-levy form dated 14 July 1922 and his signed Irish Republican Army Volunteer Reserve Agreement dated 16 July 1922. The Agreement carries the acceptance marking “Accepted. Curragh Camp.” His name is faint but legible on the signature line. Together, these papers directly document his signed and accepted enrolment in the Volunteer Reserve.

Later pages record National Forces service and attestations, service administration, medical and dental paperwork, and the end of his engagement in November 1933. Faint handwriting and conflicting entries are identified as such rather than silently resolved.

Reading the dates: The 14 and 16 July 1922 dates are handwritten and faint, but legible on the scans. The forms prove his enrolment by those dates. They do not independently establish when he first became a volunteer, including on the date of the February 1922 robbery report.

Service folio, record sheets and continuations, family and next-of-kin information, service movements, conduct and character entries, medical history and related documents. Page 17 includes a dental certificate from the 5th Infantry Battalion, Collins Barracks, dated 19 October 1929, recording William’s agreement to have dentures fitted at his own expense.

Page 18 · Voluntary levy form, July 1922

“Óglaigh na hÉireann (Volunteer Reserve) — Voluntary Levy, July 1922.” William signed the form on 14 July 1922. It records his age as 26, birthplace Templeogue, address as 59 Brookfield Buildings, Blackrock, married status, and about four and a half years’ previous British Army service. The “I.R.A. Record” table is blank.

Pages 19–21 · Agreement and personnel correspondence

Page 20 contains Personnel Records Section correspondence dated 16 October 1933 about the expiry of William’s engagement. Page 21 is the Irish Republican Army Volunteer Reserve Agreement. William’s faint signature appears on the service-offer line, the handwritten date reads 16 July 1922, and the form is marked “Accepted. Curragh Camp.”

Pages 22–23 · National Forces attestation and oath, 1924

The signed attestation is dated 26 April 1924 and identifies William with the 27th Battalion. Its accompanying oath records service from 1 April 1924 and appears to give 30 September 1928 as the end date. The handwritten birth information on this later paperwork conflicts with the 1896 civil birth record.

Pages 24–30 · Further service correspondence and discharge application

Includes an administrative correction to William’s record, service and extension paperwork, and an application for discharge. The discharge application gives expiry of term as 18 November 1933.

Pages 31–36 · Later attestation and oath, November 1926

A further signed attestation names William Metcalfe and the 27th Battalion. The oath and recruiting certification are dated 12 November 1926. Read alongside the 1924 pages, these papers show continued service documentation; they are not evidence of a fresh IRA Reserve enrolment in 1926.

Open page 31 · page 34 · page 36

Pages 37–50 · Service extensions, conduct and discharge, 1931–1933

The later section includes extension forms and related battalion correspondence. A letter dated 6 October 1933 says William would not be permitted to extend his original enlistment. Personnel Records Section correspondence dated 16 October 1933 lists his term ending on 18 November 1933. The file records the end of this engagement in 1933.

Related record outside this file

National Army Census, 1922

The family archive’s census transcription lists William as a Corporal in the Infantry, attested on 12 July 1922 at Dundalk, with paybook number 19818. This census is separate from the 50-page personnel file above.

Source and transcription notes

Source: family-supplied scan titled “VR172 William Metcalfe”, 50 scanned pages. Page links refer to the page number shown by a PDF viewer. The complete scan is retained as supplied. Handwriting is very faint in places; transcriptions on this guide are limited to readings supported by the visible page, with uncertain details described cautiously.
